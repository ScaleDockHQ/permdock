## Direction items (deferred, a product decision each)

Each needs a design decision against `PRODUCT.md` and `roadmap.mdx` before any plan is written:

- **SCIM relay store generator** (`permdock scim generate`): a store generator for multi-tenant SCIM relay deployments, grounded in `src/scim`. Effort L.
- **Agent-loop step-up wrapper** (`withStepUp`): an AI SDK / agent tool-loop wrapper that turns `approval-required` and elevated-access refusals into a step-up step. Grounded in `src/ai-sdk`, `src/core/elevated.ts` and the approvals framework. Effort M.
- **Field-masking RLS compiler** (`rls.fields: 'mask'`): emit `CASE WHEN <policy> THEN col ELSE null END` projections. Grounded in `src/cli/sql` and `rls-generate`. Effort L.

## Findings considered and rejected

Recorded so the next audit does not re-open them:

- `map-claims.ts` `invalidClaims` handling differs between `subjectFromJwt` and `subjectFromIntrospection`: behaviourally consistent (claims dropped, principal kept). Not a bug.
- `notIn` NULL semantics: identical in `src/conditions/evaluate.ts`, the NNF negation in `src/conditions/compile.ts` and the SQL translation in `src/cli/sql/conditions.ts`.
- A `WeakMap` cache so `subjectFromJwt` reuses its verifier: module-level mutable state, which invariant 7 forbids. F6 fixes the guidance instead.
- `matchWriteScope`'s asymmetric deny handling: correct fail-closed behaviour.
- `use()` called conditionally in `src/react/hooks.ts`: allowed by React.
- `$/` local GitHub Actions in `.github/workflows`: deliberate (commit `d541f9cd`).
- Break-glass deny kept in `trace.denies` when the grant lifts it: documented in `concepts/decisions.mdx` ("or the deny a break-glass grant lifted").
- Moderate `pnpm audit` advisories (`uuid`, `decode-uri-component`, `fast-uri`, `ip-address`): transitive, dev-only, below the `--audit-level high` gate. The two ignored HIGH advisories are documented in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` `auditConfig`.
- No `TODO`/`FIXME` debt in `packages/permdock/src`, `pnpm knip` clean, no `as any`, `@ts-ignore`, `eval` or `new Function` in src.
