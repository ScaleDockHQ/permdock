---
title: "0045: Drop the subject condition-ref alias"
description: The `subject` ref builder and the `subject.*` wire prefix are removed before 0.1.0; `principal` and `context` are the only condition refs. The `Role = RoleBinding` type alias goes with them.
---

## Status

Accepted, September 2026. Supersedes decision 1 of [0040](/docs/decisions/0040-principal-refs-and-question-verbs), which kept `subject` as a deprecated alias.

## Context

[0040](/docs/decisions/0040-principal-refs-and-question-verbs) split the three meanings of `subject` and made `principal` and `context` the condition-ref builders, but it kept the old `subject` builder exported and kept `subject.*` accepted as a ref prefix in evaluators, the `toWhere` compilers and the RLS generator. That deprecation window was sized for published consumers.

There are none. Nothing has shipped to npm, so the alias only costs: two spellings for one ref in every teaching page, a second prefix in every compiler branch, a `subject.*` form in the condition wire format that a snapshot or a remote decision may carry, and the `permdock rls import` emitter still writing `subject.id` into generated policies.

The same reasoning applies to `Role = RoleBinding` in `core/policy.ts`. [0038](/docs/decisions/0038-typed-vocabulary) made `Role` the vocabulary leaf type; the alias kept the pre-vocabulary meaning alive under the same name, which is worse than a rename.

## Decision

1. **`principal` and `context` are the only condition-ref builders.** The `subject` export is removed from `permdock` and from `permdock`'s condition entry.
2. **`subject.*` is not a ref prefix.** `isSubjectRef` and `resolveConditionRef` recognise `principal`, `principal.*`, `context` and `context.*` only. A `{ ref: 'subject.id' }` node is not a subject ref: it resolves to `undefined`, which fails closed like any other unresolvable value.
3. **The RLS compiler drops the prefix too.** `permdock rls generate` compiles `principal.id`, `principal.tenant` and `principal.claim.*`; `permdock rls import` emits `{ ref: 'principal.id' }`.
4. **`Role = RoleBinding` is deleted.** Grant lists take a `RoleBinding` from `role()`; `Role` is the vocabulary leaf from `defineRoles`.

Both removals land before 0.1.0, so no published version ever accepted the alias and the condition wire format has one spelling from its first release.

## Consequences

- Policies written against a pre-release checkout that used `subject.id` must rename it to `principal.id`, and `subject.context.x` to `context.x`. `pnpm exec permdock doctor` reports the resulting `undefined` refs as non-portable conditions.
- One branch per prefix disappears from `conditions/refs.ts`, `conditions/compile.ts` and `cli/rls-sql.ts`.
- The `definePolicy` option is untouched: `principal` is the name, `subject` is still accepted there ([0040](/docs/decisions/0040-principal-refs-and-question-verbs) decision 2). That alias is an option key, not a wire form, and costs nothing at the boundary.
- Roadmap open question 3 is closed rather than carried into 0.1.0.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the alias until 1.0.** Rejected: a deprecation window protects published consumers, and there are none. Shipping it would freeze `subject.*` into the 0.1.0 condition wire format for the life of the major.
- **Keep accepting `subject.*` on the wire but stop exporting the builder.** Rejected: an accepted-but-unwritable prefix is the worst of both. A remote decision or snapshot carrying it would be read by PermDock and by nothing else in the ecosystem.
- **Rename `Role` to `RoleLeaf` and keep `Role = RoleBinding`.** Rejected: the vocabulary name is the one in the public API and in [0038](/docs/decisions/0038-typed-vocabulary).
