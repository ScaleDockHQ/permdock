`mayUse(permdock, permission)` is exported from `permdock`. It answers whether some grant in the instance's snapshot could match for its subject, active tenant and delegation, and is the listing hint `permdock/mcp` and `permdock/a2a` already use. An MCP server that `protectServer` cannot wrap, such as better-supabase `createMcp`, filters its tool list with it and decides each call with `decide`; `adapters/mcp.mdx` has the recipe.
