Docs: OpenID Connect and JOSE alignment. Adds `standards/openid-connect` and `standards/jose`; specifies `permdock/jwt` OIDC Discovery (`discovery`), `typ` / `accept` handling, JWE posture (`decryptionKeys`), the RFC 9864 algorithm allow-list, RFC 6750 / RFC 9470 error mapping, the RFC 9767 `access` claim and `subjectFromIntrospection` (Phase 4); introduces the `TokenVerifier` and `TokenSigner` extension interfaces with `joseTokenVerifier` and `joseTokenSigner`; specifies JWS-signed snapshots (`permdock-snapshot+jwt`), the optional signed approval token (`permdock-approval+jwt`) and signed decision-batch export (`permdock-decisions+jwt`), resolving the Cloud key story and the snapshot `issuedAt` question; ADR 0026 adds `principal.issuer`, `principal.assurance` (`acr`, `amr`, `authTime`), `subject.session`, the `cnf`-shaped `Binding`, `on('auth')` `reason: 'invalid-token'` with a `cause`, the `insufficient-user-authentication` Decision reason and one `actor.kind` per source; `permdock/ssf` accepts OIDC Back-Channel Logout; `permdock doctor` gains PD013–PD015; folds in RFC 9767, RFC 9864 and the Interledger GNAP grant.
